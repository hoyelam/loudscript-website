Frequently asked questions

LoudScript for Mac FAQ

How do I make my Mac read selected text aloud?

Select text in any app and choose Read with LoudScript from the Services menu, or press Option-Command-R after enabling Selected Text access.

Can LoudScript read text from screenshots or images?

Yes. Capture an area of your screen and LoudScript will find and read the text. Screen Recording permission is required.

Can LoudScript read PDFs?

Yes. Import up to 50 pages at a time. LoudScript shows the original PDF beside the extracted text and highlights the current sentence.

Does LoudScript work offline?

Yes. Apple voices work on your Mac. Supertonic, Kokoro, and PocketTTS also work locally after you download their models. AI formatting may send text to your chosen AI provider when enabled.

Which Macs support LoudScript?

LoudScript requires macOS 15.6 or later. Apple voices and Supertonic support Intel and Apple silicon Macs. Kokoro and PocketTTS require Apple silicon.
