LoudScript for Mac vs iPhone and iPad

LoudScript has separate desktop and mobile editions. Choose the native Mac download for Mac workflows; choose the App Store edition for iPhone and iPad.

By Ho Ye Lam · Updated August 20, 2026

The short answer

Using a Mac? Download LoudScript for Mac from this website. It is built for desktop workflows such as reading selected text across Mac apps, screenshot OCR, PDF imports, floating playback, and local speech engines.

Using an iPhone or iPad? Get the separate App Store edition. Its store listing, purchases, compatibility, and release schedule are independent from the direct Mac edition.

Why the editions are separate

macOS, iOS, and iPadOS expose different system integrations. The Mac edition can use the Services menu, a global keyboard shortcut, Accessibility permission for selected text, Screen Recording permission for screenshot OCR, desktop windows, and a floating player.

Buying or downloading one edition should not be treated as installing the other. Use the official destination for the device in front of you.
