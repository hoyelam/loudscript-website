What is the best text-to-speech app for Mac?

The best choice depends on what you read. Start with macOS Spoken Content for a built-in shortcut; choose LoudScript when you need selected-text workflows, PDF highlighting, screenshot OCR, local voice choices, and reading history in one app.

By Ho Ye Lam · Updated August 20, 2026

Disclosure and recommendation

This guide is published on LoudScript’s website and written by LoudScript’s developer. It does not claim to be an independent ranking. The comparison uses visible product capabilities, privacy boundaries, compatibility, cost, and limitations so you can decide whether LoudScript fits.

Our recommendation: try Apple’s built-in Spoken Content first if you only need basic system speech. Try LoudScript if the reading workflows below solve problems the built-in feature does not.

Five things to compare

  1. Input: selected text, pasted text, screenshots, images, or PDFs.
  2. Reading experience: highlighting, controls, window behavior, and history.
  3. Privacy: what stays on the Mac and what optional features contact a provider.
  4. Compatibility: macOS version, processor, languages, and model downloads.
  5. Output: live listening versus creating exported narration or studio audio.

Start with macOS Spoken Content

Apple includes Spoken Content in System Settings under Accessibility. It can speak selected text with a keyboard shortcut and is the simplest option when you want no extra app, no separate reading library, and standard system voices.

Apple maintains the current setup instructions in its official Spoken Content guide.

Choose LoudScript when

  • You move between selected text, screenshots, pasted text, and PDF pages.
  • You want the current sentence highlighted in the original PDF.
  • You want a floating player while working in another window.
  • You want reading history and several local speech-engine choices.
  • You prefer a native app whose core speech workflows can operate locally.

Choose something else when

  • You only need an occasional built-in shortcut; macOS Spoken Content may already be enough.
  • You need studio narration, voice cloning, or an audio-production export workflow.
  • Your Mac cannot run macOS 15.6 or later.
  • You need a cloud voice catalog or organization-wide administration.
  • You need the iPhone or iPad edition; use the separate platform comparison.

Privacy boundary

Apple voices and LoudScript’s downloaded speech models synthesize on your Mac. Screenshot OCR uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework. LoudScript does not send raw speech text, OCR output, or source-app identity to product analytics.

Optional AI formatting is the exception: when enabled, text being formatted can be sent to the third-party AI provider you configure. Review the privacy policy before enabling it.

Try LoudScript on your Mac

LoudScript is a free, signed and notarized download for macOS 15.6 or later. It supports Intel and Apple silicon Macs, although Kokoro and PocketTTS require Apple silicon.
