LoudScript support

Get help with installation, macOS permissions, speech models, playback, history, or the LoudScript apps for Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

Contact support by email

Email hoyelam@kin-yee.com. Replies usually arrive within 24–48 hours.

Include the platform, app version, macOS or iOS version, what you expected, and what happened. Screenshots are useful when they do not contain private text.

Quick help for LoudScript on Mac

Selected text is not being read

Open LoudScript Settings → Permissions and confirm Selected Text access. The system-wide Option-Command-R workflow uses macOS Accessibility access.

Screenshot capture is unavailable

Allow LoudScript under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, then return to the app and try the capture again.

A downloadable voice is unavailable

Check the model picker for installation progress and compatibility. Kokoro and PocketTTS require Apple silicon; Apple voices and Supertonic support Intel and Apple silicon.
